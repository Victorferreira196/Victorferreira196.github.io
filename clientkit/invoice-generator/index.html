Invoice FAQ

What must a freelance invoice include?

Your name and contact, the client's name, a unique invoice number, issue and due dates, itemised work with quantities and rates, tax if you charge it, the total, and how to pay.

What payment terms should I use?

Net 7 or Net 14 is common for freelancers. Shorter terms get you paid faster; state any late fee in your contract. If a client pays late, the late fee calculator works out what you can add.

Do I need to charge tax?

It depends on your country and registration status. Set Tax % to 0 if you don't charge it, and check local rules.

Is my data stored anywhere?

Only as a draft in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
