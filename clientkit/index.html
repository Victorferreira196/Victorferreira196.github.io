Freelance proposal FAQ

What should a freelance proposal include?

The client's problem, your approach, a clear list of deliverables with prices, a timeline, payment terms (deposit and balance), revision limits and an expiry date.

Should I ask for a deposit?

Most freelancers ask for 30–50% upfront. It filters out unserious clients and protects you if the project stalls.

Is my data private?

Yes. The generator runs entirely in your browser and saves a draft only on your own device.

Can my client sign it online?

Yes. Click “Get signing link” and send it. Your client reviews the proposal, types their name to accept, and sends you back a signed copy with a document ID. No accounts needed on either side.

How do I save it as a PDF?

Click “Download PDF” and choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog.
