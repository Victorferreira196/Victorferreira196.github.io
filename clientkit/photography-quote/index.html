      What should a photography quote include?

      The shoot type, date and coverage hours, how many edited images the client gets and how they are delivered, add-ons (second shooter, video, album, drone), travel, the usage licence, the total, the deposit to book the date, when the balance is due, and your cancellation and rescheduling terms. Need a longer document for a non-photo service? Use the general proposal generator.

      How do photographers price a shoot?

      Coverage fee (hours × rate) + post-production (edited images × editing price) + add-ons + travel, then a usage fee for commercial work. Work out a rate that covers editing time, gear and quiet months with the hourly rate calculator, or build a fixed price with buffer in the project quote calculator.

      What is a usage licence and why charge for it?

      You usually keep the copyright; the client buys the right to use the images. Personal use (a family album, a wedding gallery) is included. Images used on a company website, in print marketing or in paid ads earn the client money, so commercial and advertising licences cost more. Spell the licence out in a freelance contract or a statement of work for bigger commercial jobs.

      How much deposit should I take?

      25–50% to lock in the date, non-refundable because you turn other clients away for it. Send the deposit with the invoice generator, confirm each payment with the receipt generator, and use the late fee calculator if the balance is late.
