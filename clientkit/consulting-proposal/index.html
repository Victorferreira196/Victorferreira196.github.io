            What should a consulting proposal include?

            The client’s current situation in their own words, the objectives and how success is measured, your phased approach and what each phase delivers, what is not included, the investment next to the value it should return, payment terms and expenses, the timeline, confidentiality, who owns the work, and one clear next step. For other kinds of projects use the general proposal generator.

            Fixed fee, retainer or value-based?

            A fixed project fee suits a defined piece of work like a diagnostic or a strategy — estimate the days and multiply by your day rate (the rate calculator finds it, and the project quote calculator adds a buffer). A monthly retainer suits ongoing advice with a set number of hours. Value-based pricing charges a share of the measurable value the work creates, and suits projects where that value is large and can be estimated.

            How do I show ROI?

            Estimate the yearly value the project should create — extra profit or costs saved — and divide it by your fee. A return of 5–10× makes the decision easy, and shifts the conversation from your day rate to the client’s outcome.

            What happens after the client says yes?

            Send a consulting agreement or a statement of work that repeats the scope and terms, then the deposit invoice. Send a receipt when it is paid, and add a late fee if a payment is overdue.

            Does this proposal generator store my data?

            No. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent to a server. Your inputs are saved only in your own browser so they are still there next time.
