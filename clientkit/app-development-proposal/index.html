      What should a software development proposal include?

      The client’s goal, the tech stack, a feature list with estimated effort, what is out of scope, milestones with dates, the price broken down, a payment schedule tied to milestones, a bug-fix warranty and one clear next step. For a general proposal for any service, use the proposal generator; for websites, the web design proposal.

      How do I estimate the price of an app?

      List every feature, estimate hours for each, add 15–25% for project management and QA, multiply by your hourly rate and round. Not sure what rate to use? Try the hourly rate calculator. For a quick fixed-price number with contingency buffer, use the project quote calculator.

      Why show hours per feature?

      When the budget is tight, the client can drop features instead of asking you to cut your rate. Untick a feature here and the price, milestones and timeline update instantly.

      How should payments be split on a development project?

      A deposit of 25–50% at signing and the rest across milestones (beta delivery, launch) keeps you from working far ahead of payment. Put it in a freelance contract or a detailed statement of work, invoice each milestone with the invoice generator, and use the late fee calculator if a payment slips.

      What is a bug-fix warranty?

      A fixed period after launch (often 30–90 days) in which you fix defects in the delivered features at no charge. New features and changes are not bugs — those go into a new quote or the monthly support plan.
