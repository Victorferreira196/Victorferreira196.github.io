How to set your freelance rate

Why is my rate so much higher than a salary divided by hours?

Freelancers pay their own taxes, tools, insurance and time off, and only part of the week is billable — admin, sales and learning are unpaid. A rate that ignores this quietly pays you less than a job.

What billable share is realistic?

Many freelancers bill 50–70% of the hours they work. New freelancers, who spend more time finding clients, are usually at the lower end.

Should I charge hourly or per project?

Use the hourly rate as your floor, then quote per project or per value delivered whenever you can estimate the work reliably.

Is this tax advice?

No. Use your effective overall tax rate as an estimate and confirm with a local accountant.
