Freelance contracts: common questions

What should a freelance contract include?

Who the parties are, the scope of work, fees and payment schedule, what happens if payment is late, who owns the work and when, confidentiality, how either side can end the project, and both signatures. Put the detailed deliverables in a statement of work and reference it here.

What is a kill fee?

The share of the total fee the client pays if they cancel after the project has started. It compensates you for reserved time and work already done; 25–50% is common. The deposit usually counts toward it.

When should intellectual property transfer?

A fair default is that ownership of the final deliverables passes to the client once you have been paid in full. Until then you keep the rights — your strongest protection against non-payment.

How much should I charge?

Use the rate calculator to find the hourly floor that covers taxes, costs and unbilled time, then bill the deposit and milestones with the invoice generator.

Is this contract legally binding?

A signed agreement is generally enforceable, but laws differ by country and state. This is a template, not legal advice — have a local lawyer review it for high-value or unusual projects.
