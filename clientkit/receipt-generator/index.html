Payment receipts for freelancers

What is the difference between an invoice and a receipt?

An invoice asks the client to pay. A receipt confirms that a payment was received: it shows the amount paid, the date, the payment method and any balance still due. If the client is paying late, work out any agreed fee first with the late fee calculator.

What should a payment receipt include?

Your name or business, the client, a receipt number, the payment date, what was paid for, the amount and currency, tax if any, the payment method and reference, the invoice it relates to, and the remaining balance.

Can I issue a receipt for a deposit or partial payment?

Yes. Enter the amount actually received in “Amount paid”. The receipt is marked PARTIALLY PAID and shows the balance due — useful for the deposit set in your contract or SOW.

How do I save the receipt as a PDF?

Click “Print / Save as PDF” and choose “Save as PDF” as the printer. Only the receipt is printed, not the form.

Is this a tax document?

It is a simple proof of payment. Some countries require specific fields (tax IDs, sequential numbering, official e-invoices). Check local rules — this tool is not tax or legal advice.

Does this receipt generator store my data?

No. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent to a server. Your inputs are saved only in your own browser so they are still there next time.
