        What should a social media management proposal include?

        The client’s goal, which platforms you manage, how many posts per week, extra services (community management, stories and short video, ads, reporting), what is not included, the monthly retainer, any setup fee, the minimum term, the notice period and one clear next step. For a one-off project in any field, use the general proposal generator; for websites, the web design proposal generator.

        How much should I charge for social media management?

        A common structure is a monthly fee per platform plus add-ons. Estimate the hours each platform takes per month (planning, design, captions, scheduling, replies) and multiply by your target hourly rate — the rate calculator helps you find it.

        Should I charge a setup fee?

        Yes, if the first month includes an audit, strategy and building the first content calendar. Bill it with the first month, in advance, on an invoice.

        Why a minimum term and notice period?

        Social media results take time, and a 3-month minimum protects both sides. A notice period (often 30 days) avoids losing a month’s income without warning. Put both in a freelance contract and charge a late fee if invoices are paid late.

        Is ad spend included?

        No — ad spend is paid by the client directly to the platform. Your ads-management fee covers setting up, monitoring and optimising campaigns.

        Does this proposal generator store my data?
